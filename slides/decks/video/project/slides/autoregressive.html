The model

It predicts one token at a time

PassReadsSamples
1The capital of France" is"
2The capital of France is" Paris"
3The capital of France is Paris"."
4The capital of France is Paris.(end)

Each sampled token is appended to what it reads, and it predicts again, until it predicts that it should stop. This is autoregression: TokensOut = Model(TokensIn).

harness engineering · agent primitives

Real tokens, spaces included. Each pass reads everything before it, including what it just wrote. That's the whole job of a model.
