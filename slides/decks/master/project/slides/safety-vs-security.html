Safety & Security

What could do harm, and who could make it?

Safety

Guardrails against the agent's own mistakes: a box that sees only this folder, no network, and nothing runs without your yes.

Security

Guardrails against someone turning it against you: text it reads, secrets in its folder, a poisoned memory, a tampered image.

Both are guardrails, for different purposes. The guards that matter sit in the harness, where no text can talk them out of their job.

harness engineering · production · safety & security

One concern, two halves. Safety assumes a well-meaning agent that can still make mistakes. Security assumes an adversary who can put text in front of it.
